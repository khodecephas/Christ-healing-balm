A Ministry of Faith, Hope and Transformation

Christ Healing Balm Ministry Tabernacle of Faith International exists to point every person to Jesus Christ, the healer of hearts and the giver of rest. Rooted in the Gospel, we gather to worship, pray, study the Word and walk together in love, so that lives are restored and faith grows.

Whether you are new to faith or have walked with God for years, there is a place for you here.
